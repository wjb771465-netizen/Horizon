# World and general news profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding public affairs, geopolitics, diplomacy, conflict, elections, governance, society, or general current events rather than technology products or financial markets.

Typical items include international relations, wars and ceasefires, sanctions, treaties, summits, elections, legislation with broad civic impact, natural disasters with major human consequences, public-health emergencies, and major social or institutional developments reported by general news outlets.

Route company earnings, markets, monetary policy, and investment-focused coverage to the financial news profile. Route product releases, research results, software updates, security incidents, and engineering developments to the technology news profile. Do not route celebrity gossip, sports scores, lifestyle tips, or thinly sourced rumors here.
